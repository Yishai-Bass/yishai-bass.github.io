Run orders from a live dashboard

Launch without rebuilding your menu by hand

Register your shop, set hours and bank details, upload your logo, and use AI-assisted menu setup from photos, gallery images, or PDFs.

Keep the counter in control

Message customers, reject with reasons, handle out-of-stock items, pause curbside, and disable unavailable menu choices when service gets tight.

Operations

Built with baristas to maximise efficiency.

Give the team fewer scattered decisions during a rush, with clear controls for orders, availability, and customer updates.

Notifications that say what matters

Manager alerts include order details, pickup names, notes, large-order signals, ETA warnings, and customer arrival cues.

Availability and curbside controls

Mark items or choices unavailable, temporarily disable curbside, auto re-enable it later, and auto-close the store when hours end.

Customer conversation when something changes

Send a message before cancelling, ask about substitutions, and keep the order history clear for both sides.
