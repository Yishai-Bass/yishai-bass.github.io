Frequently asked questions

Is OrderUp! live?

Yes. The app is live, with availability depending on participating coffee shops and the features enabled for each location.

How do coffee shops get started?

Book a demo and share your shop details. The team can help with registration, menu setup, operating controls, payouts, and launch planning.

What does OrderUp! do for me?

Earn Bean Bucks, skip the queue, order ahead. Send vouchers and gift cards. Order as a group.

What does OrderUp! do for coffee shop operators?

Registration, AI menu setup, live orders, status controls, customer messaging, availability tools, curbside handling, stats, tips, and settlement visibility.

Who can order from a workplace coffee shop?

Only approved employees can access and order from it. The shop is marked as employees-only and stays hidden from public users.

Can we send coffee vouchers to employees?

Yes. Digital vouchers can be issued for individuals, teams, recognition, meetings, events, or a wider workplace coffee treat.
